# Seat billing

Source: https://cal.com/help/billing-and-usage/seat-billing.md (fetched 2026-08-10, real Cal.com help article)

> Understand how seat-based billing works for teams and organizations on Cal.com.

Cal.com charges teams and organizations according to the number of seats utilized during each billing period. Your billing settings will display one of two models: **high water mark billing** or **active user billing**.

## High water mark billing

Under high water mark (HWM) billing, charges are based on the **highest member count reached** during your billing cycle, not merely the final count.

Example: If your team has 10 members initially, reaches 15 mid-cycle, then decreases to 12, you pay for 15 seats.

### What you see in billing settings

The **Seat billing** section displays:

* **Current members**: your team's present member count
* **Peak seats this period**: the highest member count recorded since the billing period started
* **Billed seats**: the number of seats you are charged for (the higher of current members or peak seats, with a minimum of 1)

The billing period's start date is visible for reference.

## Active user billing

This model charges based on team members who **participated in bookings** (either hosting or attending) throughout the period.

### What you see in billing settings

The **Active users** section shows:

* **Total members**: all team roster members
* **Active users**: the number of members who hosted or attended at least one booking during the period
* **Minimum seats commitment**: appears if your plan includes this requirement
* **Billed seats**: the higher of your active user count or minimum seat commitment

A table lists each active user with email and role (host or attendee). Selecting a user displays their bookings.

## Viewing your seat billing details

1. Go to billing settings: access **Team Settings** or **Organization Settings**, then select **Billing**.
2. Review seat information: find the **Seat billing** or **Active users** section to review current usage and billed seats.
3. Manage your subscription: select **Billing portal** to access Stripe for payment updates, invoice viewing, or subscription modifications.

Note: Seat billing details only appear for teams and organizations on a paid plan.

## How seat changes are tracked

Member additions and removals are logged automatically. For HWM billing, the system verifies whether new member counts surpass the previous peak. Seat tracking restarts each billing cycle.
