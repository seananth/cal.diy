# Add New Availability

Source: https://cal.com/help/availabilities/add-new-availability.md (fetched 2026-08-10, real Cal.com help article)

This guide explains how to establish a new availability schedule within your Cal.com account.

## Steps to Create a New Schedule

1. Locate and click the "+ New" button positioned in the upper right area of the screen
2. A popup window will appear on your screen
3. Enter your desired name for the availability schedule in the provided field
4. Select the "Continue" button to proceed
5. Your new schedule will be created and you'll be redirected to its settings page

## Settings Configuration

Once created, each availability schedule has its own dedicated settings page where you can:

- Specify which days and hours apply to this schedule
- Select the relevant timezone
- Configure any date overrides
- Access troubleshooting resources

**Important:** Remember to click the save button in the upper right corner after making any changes.
